# Module 1: Pre-Calculus Foundations

## Overview

This module provides essential mathematical foundations needed for calculus. You'll review fundamental concepts about numbers, algebra, and coordinate geometry that form the building blocks for everything that follows in calculus.

**Modules in this learning path:**
1. [Real Line, Decimals and Significant Figures](#1-real-line-decimals-and-significant-figures)

4. [Equations and Inequalities](#4-equations-and-inequalities)
5. [Sign Diagrams](#5-sign-diagrams)
6. [Distance and Absolute Value](#6-distance-and-absolute-value)
7. [Coordinate Systems](#7-coordinate-systems)

## 1. Real Line, Decimals and Significant Figures

Introduces the number line, understanding decimal representation, and the concept of significant figures—crucial for accurate mathematical communication.

**Key Topics:**
- Understanding the real number line
- Decimal notation and place values
- Significant figures and rounding
- Scientific notation

📄 **Lesson Material:** [01 - Real Line, Decimals and Significant Figures.pdf](./01%20-%20Real%20Line,%20Decimals%20and%20Significant%20Figures.pdf)

## 2. Algebraic Expressions

Covers manipulation of algebraic expressions, expanding brackets, factoring, and simplifying complex expressions.

**Key Topics:**
- Simplifying algebraic expressions
- Expanding brackets and distributive property
- Factoring techniques
- Working with fractions and rational expressions

📄 **Lesson Material:** [02 - Algebraic Expressions.pdf](./02%20-%20Algebraic%20Expressions.pdf)

## 3. Theorem of Pythagoras and Square Root of Two

Explores the Pythagorean theorem and its implications, including discovering the square root of two as an irrational number.

**Key Topics:**
- Pythagorean theorem (a² + b² = c²)
- Calculating distances in right triangles
- Irrational numbers
- The historical significance of √2

📄 **Lesson Material:** [03 - Theorem of Pythagoras and Square Root of Two.pdf](./03%20-%20Theorem%20of%20Pythagoras%20and%20Square%20Root%20of%20Two.pdf)

## 4. Equations and Inequalities

Master solving linear and quadratic equations, understanding solution methods, and working with inequalities.

**Key Topics:**
- Solving linear equations
- Solving quadratic equations
- Solution by factoring and quadratic formula
- Inequality notation and solving inequalities

## 5. Sign Diagrams

Learn to create and use sign diagrams to determine where expressions are positive or negative—essential for understanding function behavior.

**Key Topics:**
- Creating sign diagrams for linear expressions
- Creating sign diagrams for products and quotients
- Using sign diagrams to solve inequalities
- Applications in calculus

📄 **Lesson Material:** [05 - Sign Diagrams.pdf](./05%20-%20Sign%20Diagrams.pdf)

## 6. Distance and Absolute Value

Understand absolute value as a distance concept and its applications in equations and inequalities.

**Key Topics:**
- Definition of absolute value
- Geometric interpretation: distance
- Solving equations with absolute values
- Solving inequalities with absolute values

📄 **Lesson Material:** [06 - Distance and Absolute Value.pdf](./06%20-%20Distance%20and%20Absolute%20Value.pdf)

**Key Topics:**

## 8. Lines and Circles in the Plane

Study the fundamental geometric objects in the coordinate plane: lines and circles.

**Key Topics:**
- Equation of a line (slope-intercept form, point-slope form)

## How to Use This Module

This module is best studied sequentially as each topic builds on previous ones. Take time to work through the examples and practice problems in each section. A strong foundation here will make calculus concepts much more accessible.
